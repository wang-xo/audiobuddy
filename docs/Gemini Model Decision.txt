Gemini Model Decision

Decision date: 1 September 2026
Review date: 1 January 2027

Decision:

Use gemini-3.6-flash as the primary AudioBuddy summarization model.
Use gemini-3.7-flash only as the fallback for transient 3.6 failures.

Reasoning:

The previous order used gemini-3.7-flash first. During controlled live testing
on 1 September 2026, both tested audio files uploaded successfully in about
1.7 seconds, but gemini-3.7-flash returned InternalServerError after about
48 to 50 seconds. gemini-3.6-flash then completed both summaries successfully
in about 10 to 28 seconds.

A text-only gemini-3.6-flash request also succeeded in about 9 seconds. This
indicates that the immediate reliability issue is gemini-3.7-flash generation,
not the API key, account credit, file upload, or local audio processing.

Review this order on 1 January 2027, or sooner if Gemini 3.7 Flash becomes
consistently reliable in controlled AudioBuddy tests.
